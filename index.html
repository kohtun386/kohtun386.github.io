  • Tenant isolation

    Every policy gates on factory_id = current_factory_id(), resolved from active membership — and factory_id is immutable across governed tables.

  • RPC-only writes

    Production records carry select-only policies. Clients cannot insert, update, or delete directly; all governed writes flow through approval and logging RPCs.

  • Immutable records

    Approved production logs are locked — only payment status may change. audit_logs rejects updates and deletes, so the trail cannot be rewritten.

  • Payroll states

    Piece-rate payroll moves through DRAFT → CALCULATED → FINALIZED → PAID. Invalid transitions are rejected by trigger, not by UI convention.

  • Domain depth

    Production logging, piece-rate payroll, subcontracting, material allocation, stock movements, purchasing, sales, and financial reporting — not a CRUD demo.

  • Factory-floor UX

    Mobile-first screens for shared tablets, with role-based supervisor, operator, and owner flows. English and Burmese interface support.

  • Test surface

    75 test files across Vitest and Playwright, covering approval paths, state transitions, and core operational workflows.

  • Atomic checkout

    checkout_complete recomputes the total server-side — a tampered client amount is ignored — then reserves invoice numbers, deducts stock, and updates customer stats in one transaction.

  • 9 payment methods

    KBZPay, WavePay, AYAPay, CBPay, MPU, cash, card, credit, and digital — with split payments and customer credit limits, built for Myanmar payment reality.

  • Tiered SaaS

    Free / Growth / Pro limits are enforced server-side: a product cap with row-lock race protection and daily-order limits with SELECT … FOR UPDATE.

  • Role-aware RLS

    platform_admin never appears in a client policy — it operates only through service-role Edge Functions. Privileged RPCs are revoked from public execution.

  • Implicit deny

    audit_logs has zero client policies — implicit deny. Only Edge Functions write, capturing request origin for the trail.

  • Counter-ready PWA

    Installable on iPad and Android for shared shop counters, with inventory, purchasing, discounts, CSV import/export, and sales reporting.

  • Tested

    22 test files and roughly 129 test blocks across Vitest, React Testing Library, and Playwright — including payment, checkout, and permission paths.
